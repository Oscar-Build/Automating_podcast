# EstateDocPrep Educational Channel — Topic Bank

**Host:** Oscar Vasquez, founder of EstateDocPrep, a California Registered & Bonded Legal Document Assistant (LDA) with 15 years of experience in estate planning. Core message: making estate planning easy and affordable.

**Channels:** estatedocprep (English), estatedocprepenespanol (Spanish) — same topics, native (not translated) Spanish scripts.

Every topic below maps to a theme-wheel day (see channel config). This is a living reference, not a locked schedule — new topics get added as issues via the Topic Idea Intake form.

## Monday — Famous & Regretful (celebrities/public figures who died without a trust, what it cost their families)
- Prince: no will, no trust — the multi-year estate battle
- Aretha Franklin: handwritten wills found after death
- Any current news story matching this pattern (breaking-news interrupt slot)

## Tuesday — Famous & Protected (public figures who planned well)
- Examples of celebrities whose estates transferred smoothly because of proper trust planning

## Wednesday — Everyday Probate Story (8-10 min narrative, not a celebrity — a relatable California family)
- What happens when a family has to go through probate court after a parent dies without a trust
- A blended family without a trust: who actually inherits under California law
- The cost and timeline of probate vs. what a trust would have avoided

## Thursday — Watch-Outs (25 mistakes people make)
- Funding mistakes: creating a trust but never transferring the house into it
- Letting a Healthcare Directive go stale (hospitals prefer directives under ~3 years old)
- Letting a Power of Attorney become unworkable/outdated
- Not updating beneficiaries after divorce or remarriage
- Assuming a will avoids probate (it does not)
- (Build out to a full 25-item list over time, one per week)

## Friday — Real Questions (from viewers, via the Comment Harvest Q&A bank)

## Saturday — How It Works (educational deep-dives)
- Comprehensive vs. non-comprehensive living trust: what is actually included
- What a living trust actually costs in California in 2026, and why attorney pricing varies
- Revocable vs. irrevocable trusts: which one most families need
